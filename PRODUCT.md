# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Local residents in Malang looking for an authentic, traditional Javanese drink.

## Product Purpose
To offer a high-quality, authentic version of the traditional Javanese Sinom drink (tamarind leaves, turmeric, palm sugar) that honors local heritage while presenting a premium experience. Success means becoming a trusted, go-to local brand for traditional refreshment.

## Positioning
Authentic, small-batch traditional Sinom brewed with just three natural ingredients (tamarind leaves, turmeric, palm sugar) without artificial preservatives. It is positioned as a premium, heritage-rich product rather than a cheap street-side commodity.

## Operating Context
Consumers ordering via Instagram (`@sinomaramalang`), receiving fresh batches brewed weekly in Malang.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must link to the real Instagram (`@sinomaramalang`) for orders.
- Must accurately represent the three ingredients (tamarind leaves, turmeric, palm sugar).

## Brand Commitments
- Tone is premium and heritage-focused.
- Visual language established (dark mode, amber `#c9a96e` accent, Outfit font) is a confirmed brand commitment.

## Evidence on Hand
- Instagram profile link (`https://www.instagram.com/sinomaramalang/`).
- Factual ingredient list (Young Tamarind Leaves, Fresh Turmeric, Palm Sugar).

## Product Principles
1. **Authenticity over Flash:** The product's real ingredients and heritage speak for themselves; avoid gamified or overly modern visual tropes that distract from the tradition.
2. **Premium Simplicity:** Keep the interface focused on the essential elements (the drink, the ingredients, the order CTA) without clutter.
3. **Local Pride:** Emphasize the connection to Malang and Javanese brewing traditions.
